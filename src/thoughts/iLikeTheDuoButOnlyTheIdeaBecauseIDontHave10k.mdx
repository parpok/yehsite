# I really like the iPhone Duo. 
Seriously, I think the ideas behind it are perfect.

- Passport style shape 
When I was trying it at the shop it was really handy. Also when open it becomes even better - especially when I mention something later. Like its nice to hold in one hand. Also apps - when they'll get build to support the display configurations can work real well. 

- Pen support
I love the idea of it. Especially that it works on both displays, and its the standard Apple Pencil with USB-C that you can use with iPads. I lowkey wonder if third party pencils also work? This and the shape of the phone being simmilar to a small A6 notepad makes this phone a perfect machine for journalling. Lovely in my opinion, so many oppurtunities, from journaling, to even doodling - if I had this phone I'd probably splurge for the iPhone version of Procreate just because even if I already have the iPad version purchased. 

- Matte display is cool
Its cool, especially for content like books, or reading. Some will hate the washed colors, but I think its nice because foldable displays are just fancy plastic mirrors when looked from some angles and matte - it gives it somewhat premium feel for the sake of being different. Even if this argument sounds like BS - I just like it 

- ITS AVAILABLE WHERE I LIVE
You can actually get it officialy in Europe where I live (Poland). Usually foldables that aren't Samsung tend to be unavailable or released so quietly with bare any coverage.

## But things aren't perfect.

The duo is a first gen product and it will have pitfalls. 

Sure thats obvious but when Mr. Termus said they wanted to perfect everything about Foldables it should be perfect and not have issues with things that other folds managed to fix. Like seriously the hinge is cool for having its flex mode, but from for example iFixit its pretty easy to say that the overengineered hinge is wrecking the display.
