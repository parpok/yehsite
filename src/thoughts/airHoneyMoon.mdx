# I've got an iPhone Air 

Its a cool phone, really thin. I got it used thanks to a friend of mine - or rather my SOs friend.

Even tho its display was rather broken it was still perfectly usable. I'm upgrading from iPhone 13 so the upgrade was quite sizeable. Maybe not super big like from 6s to 13. But still this phone had many upgrades that are nice.

- Thin - its this phones main selling phone, its really thin, I like it. Even tho it has a big 6.5 inch display its really handy. Even my friends who love Mini iPhones said that the Air is handy. It gives off vibes of old Plus iPhones from the home button era which weren't literally brick-like nowadays Plus or Pro Max models but also where they had big screen - check, were thin - check, battery was good - depends - recently I've been wrecking my Air so its 50/50 - even compared to my iPhone 13 with its 3rd party battery replacement

- Smooth 120Hz display - it feels nice, it makes Liquid Glass look and feel like Apple probably wanted to. I mean they probably made it for this phone. 

- 12 GB of Ram from 4 GB - This is a giant upgrade. literally 3 times as much of Ram compared to my previous iPhone. OFC this was made so that this phone supports the full blown version of Apple Intelligence. But still thats nice. Especially that on my 13 some heavier apps could be killed instantly when going to a different app for example which is annoying as fuck

- Dynamic island - I know this is mainly a quirk - and its available since 14 Pros. But I like these gizmos, Sure live activities are there for quite a long time, but dynamic island gives them a place where they can shine if developers make them work well. I think my favourite is Music since it gives music controls everywhere by just holding on it. Another activity I like is Discord letting me control my input into the voice chat. I know Flightly has an amazing live activity but I'd need to fly somewhere and I don't think I'd fly soon so hmmm. Theres also Koleo but I don't like it. Timer to train departure feels more like something stress inducing and also why its only visible in the dynamic island and on Mac, nand during train ride status looks so ugly its meh. Also I noticed the App doesn't update often enough to reflect switching of the trains for example but that might be PKP skill issue lowkey. - wish Koleo put more effort into this because its meh at best, and design wise looks like beta product and not prime mobile development.

- Siri that works instantly - Its all because of Apple Intelligence, I know its not the best AI, far from it, from its floppy launch, to the remake in iOS 27 that I can't even play with because Apple can't deal with the EU - but its actually well implemented. As in - its Siri, it does Siri things, but the Apple Intelligence parts give it steroids, its really nice especially that most of my siri prompts are just `play some music` and variations of it. Instead of waiting, its instant - because it runs locally on my phone and not some datacenter. Sure give it a harder question and it will not understand things but I'd let it pass since Europe for now only can really use the pre iOS 27 version of Siri - Also no Polish - ffs Apple I know it might be hard but it would be easier with all the AI you're dancing with. or even better, Just understand polish content like messages, like fully, not like computing it really long like on my M1 MacBook Air. Like for me Siri is just a glorified music controller since it won't understand anything remotely Polish, and even if I type it in it will be so clunky its just easier to do everything by myself.

Overall I love this phone. Its nice. Its an upgrade from the 13 which I feel aged meh. Also to be real 13 was made in the time where iPhones were just money printers where changes were incremental with barely any cool features. - Like what was 13s killer feature - Cinematic mode?. okay ProMotion - but only on Pros. YES I know Air is a special phone compared to standard 17 and 17 Pro but it actually feels like something unusual compared to just a brick made to scroll socials, call and text with longevity for long.
I like my air - bet after honeymoon phase ill say something different.
